Models and pricing

Every model. One key.

Kovan itself is free during early access. What you pay for is the AI models your agents use: with your own OpenRouter key at the provider’s price, through Kovan at that price × 1.05 (charged to a card, with a monthly limit you set), or nothing extra when Claude Code or Codex runs on your own plan.

Prices below are in US dollars per million tokens (a token is about three quarters of a word; a model is charged for what it reads, “input”, and what it writes, “output”). On our small test tasks a whole task cost from about $0.001 (GPT-5.6 Luna) to $0.03 (Claude Sonnet 5); bigger tasks cost more.

Not sure? Start with one of these

  • JevRouter (Kovan’s default): picks a model for each step, cheap for easy ones, stronger for hard ones.
  • Claude Sonnet 5: strong at code, mid-priced.
  • GPT-5.6 Luna: very cheap and good enough for small, clear tasks.
