Models and pricing

Every model. One key.

Kovan reaches models through OpenRouter. Bring your own key and pay the provider’s price, or use managed usage at the provider’s price × 1.05, billed daily. Prices are USD per million tokens.

Prices come from the provider catalog and can change. Some models charge more for very long prompts or for cached tokens; what a run costs is shown in the app as it happens.
