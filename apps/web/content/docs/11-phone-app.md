# The phone app

The Kovan phone app (iPhone and Android) follows your team's work while you're away from your desk. Agents don't run on the phone: they run on a computer, yours or a teammate's, and the phone shows them and answers them.

## What it does

- **Needs you.** The home screen lists what waits for you across your workspaces: an agent asking for a yes, a Swarm task waiting for approval, a teammate's work handed to you. Only what your role lets you answer is listed.
- **Approve or deny** right there, or from the notification itself (it asks for Face ID or your passcode first). Commands that are hard to undo, such as a force push or deleting files, say so and ask once more.
- **Every session, live**: each agent's work as it happens, the tasks, the changes file by file (you can comment on a line), and the activity.
- **Swarm**: its state and budget, its chat, and the questions its agents ask a person. You can answer them from the phone.
- **Ask your agent** to do something: it starts on your desktop, so Kovan needs to be open there.

## Notifications

Account → Notifications lists each kind with a switch. They're sent only while you're away from your desktop (its window isn't in use).

"Your team's questions" is off at first: turn it on to be asked about teammates' agents too, when your role lets you answer them.

## Getting it

The app is on its way to the App Store and Google Play; [Download](/download/#phone) has the links once it's there. Sign in with the same account as on your desktop: email, Apple or GitHub.

You can change your name in Account; teammates see it on your work.
