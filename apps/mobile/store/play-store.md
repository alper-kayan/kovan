# Google Play listing

What goes into the Play Console for the Android app (package `dev.alperkayan.kovan`). The limits
are Google's. The texts follow the App Store listing (`app-store.md`) so both stores say the same.

## Store listing

- **App name** (30): `Kovan`
- **Short description** (80): `Watch, answer and steer your team's AI coding agents from your phone.`
- **Category:** Tools. **Tags:** Developer tools, Productivity.
- **Contact:** website `https://kovan.alperkayan.dev/`, email `kovan@alperkayan.dev`.
- **Privacy policy:** `https://kovan.alperkayan.dev/privacy/`
- **Price:** free, no in-app purchases (models paid through Kovan are billed on the web, never in
  the app).

**Full description** (4000): the App Store description, as it is, with "iPhone" read as
"Android" ("Kovan on Android doesn't run agents or edit code itself…").

## Data safety

The same data as the App Store's App Privacy section: name and email, user ID and the push token
(app functionality), what people write (prompts, comments, messages; app functionality),
crash reports. Nothing is shared with third parties for advertising; data is encrypted in
transit; people can delete their account in the app (Account → Delete account) or on the web.

## Content rating

Questionnaire: no violence, sexuality, gambling or controlled substances. User-generated
content shared between users: yes, limited to the members of a workspace. Expected: Everyone /
PEGI 3.
